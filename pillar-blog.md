### H3: Acne & Pigmentation Treatment

Acne, acne scars, and pigmentation (dark spots, melasma, uneven tone) are among the most common reasons people visit a dermatologist in Kochi. Treatments range from topical medication and chemical peels to laser-based therapies, depending on severity.

### H3: Hair Fall Treatment

Hair fall can be caused by genetics, stress, nutritional deficiencies, hormonal changes, or scalp infections. A proper diagnosis is the first step — clinics typically offer scalp analysis, blood tests (if needed), and treatments ranging from medicated solutions to PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy.

### H3: PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) Therapy

PRP therapy is a popular non-surgical option for hair regrowth, using your own blood plasma to stimulate hair follicles. It's commonly recommended for early-to-moderate hair thinning.

### H3: Laser Hair Removal

For those looking for long-term reduction in unwanted body or facial hair, laser hair removal is one of the most requested cosmetic treatments. It typically requires multiple sessions spaced a few weeks apart.

## H2: What to Expect During Your First Visit

1. **Consultation** — The dermatologist reviews your concern, medical history, and lifestyle factors.
2. **Diagnosis** — Depending on the issue, this may include a visual skin/scalp examination, dermoscopy, or lab tests.
3. **Treatment Plan** — You'll receive a personalized plan, including expected timelines, number of sessions, and costs.
4. **Follow-Up** — Most treatments require follow-up visits to track progress and adjust the plan if needed.

## H2: Frequently Asked Questions

**Is it necessary to see a dermatologist for hair fall, or can home remedies work?**
Mild hair fall can sometimes improve with lifestyle changes and over-the-counter products, but persistent or sudden hair fall should be evaluated by a dermatologist to rule out underlying causes.

**How many sessions are needed for visible results in skin treatments?**
This varies by treatment and individual skin condition — some treatments show results in 2-3 sessions, while others (like pigmentation correction) may take longer.

**Is laser hair removal painful?**
Most people describe it as a mild discomfort rather than pain, and modern lasers include cooling features to improve comfort.
