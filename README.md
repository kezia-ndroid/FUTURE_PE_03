# SEO Content Pack — Skin & Hair Clinic, Kochi

This repository contains a complete SEO content pack for a skin and hair clinic located in Kochi, Kerala — built as a content cluster (1 pillar blog + 5 supporting blogs) following the pillar/cluster SEO model used by professional SEO agencies.

## Files in This Pack

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `keyword-strategy.md` | Business overview, keyword research, content cluster map, search intent breakdown, and local SEO notes |
| `pillar-blog.md` | Main SEO article — "Best Skin & Hair Clinic in Kochi — Services, Cost & What to Expect" |
| `supporting-files.md` | All 5 supporting blogs in one file, each targeting a specific cluster keyword |

## Content Cluster Overview

**Pillar Blog**
- Best Skin & Hair Clinic in Kochi — Services, Cost & What to Expect
- Target keyword: *best skin and hair clinic in Kochi*

**Supporting Blogs**
1. Hair Fall Treatment Cost in Kochi — What You Should Know
2. PRP Hair Loss Treatment — Is It Safe?
3. How to Choose the Right Dermatologist in Kochi
4. Acne & Pigmentation Treatment in Kochi — Options & What Works
5. Laser Hair Removal Cost & Side Effects — A Practical Guide

Each supporting blog targets a specific search intent (cost research, safety concerns, decision-making, service-specific, etc.) and links back to the pillar blog. The pillar blog links out to each supporting blog from its relevant section.

## How AI Helped Scale This

AI (Claude) was used to:
- Generate SEO-friendly outlines (H1-H3) based on keyword and intent research
- Write long-form, readable blog content for all 6 articles
- Suggest an internal linking structure across the cluster
- Adapt tone, structure, and references for local (Kochi-based) SEO
- Ensure each article ends with a clear, business-focused call-to-action

## Reusability

This same structure (1 pillar + 3-5 supporting blogs, organized as a cluster around a primary keyword) can be reused for any local business by:
1. Swapping the business type, services, and location
2. Running fresh keyword research for that niche/city
3. Updating the content cluster map and search intent table accordingly
4. Regenerating content using the same prompt structure
