# SEO Content Pack — Keyword & Content Cluster Strategy

**Business:** Skin & Hair Clinic
**Location:** Kochi, Kerala
**Niche:** Dermatology / Cosmetic Skin & Hair Care Clinic

---

## 1. Business Overview

This content pack is designed for a skin and hair clinic operating in Kochi, offering services such as acne treatment, skin brightening/glow treatments, hair fall treatment, PRP therapy, hair transplant consultations, laser hair removal, and dermatologist consultations.

The goal of this content cluster is to:
- Rank for "[service] in Kochi" type searches
- Build trust and authority around the clinic's dermatologists
- Drive consultation bookings and walk-ins
- Capture both informational (research-stage) and commercial (ready-to-book) search intent

---

## 2. Keyword Research Summary

Research method: Google autosuggest, "People Also Ask" boxes, Google Trends, and AnswerThePublic, searched around core terms like "skin clinic Kochi," "hair fall treatment Kochi," and "dermatologist Kochi."

## 5. Local SEO Notes

- Each piece naturally references "Kochi" along with neighborhood-level mentions (e.g., Kakkanad, Edappally, MG Road) where relevant, to capture hyperlocal searches.
- Pillar blog includes a section addressing "near me" type intent.
- Content emphasizes local trust signals: clinic experience, dermatologist qualifications, and patient-friendly explanations.
- Recommended (outside this content pack): Google Business Profile optimization, local citations, and patient review collection — these support the on-page SEO done here.
